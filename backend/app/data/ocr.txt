Medicine Usage Guidelines
Pulmoclear Tablet
Take once daily, preferably in the evening, after meals
to minimize gastrointestinal side effects.
Swallow the whole tablet with a glass of water. Do not
crush, chew, or break it.
Quit smoking and limit caffeinated drinks to avoid lung
irritation and worsening of breathing problems.
Benz Pearls Capsule
Swallow the whole capsule with a glass of water. Do
not crush or break it.
Take three times a day or as advised by your doctor.
Can be taken with or without food.
Avoid alcohol as it increases dizziness and drowsiness.
Common Side Effects
Pulmoclear Tablet: Stomach Ache, Vomiting, Loose And
Watery Stools, Constipation, Nausea, Heart Burn
